### 6. Multi-Profile Family Accounts

**Headline**: One App, Multiple Users — Complete Privacy for Everyone

**Features**:

- **Unlimited Profiles** — Create separate accounts for each family member
- **PIN Protection** — Secure individual profiles with 4-6 digit PINs
- **Private & Shared Budgets** — Control exactly what each profile can see
- **Activity Logging** — Track who made changes and when
- **Auto-Lock** — Profiles lock automatically after inactivity
- **Lockout Protection** — Failed PIN attempts trigger temporary lockout
- **Color-Coded Avatars** — Instantly identify profiles with personalized colors

**Privacy Controls**:

- Keep personal spending completely private from other household members
- Share household budgets while maintaining individual privacy
- Each profile sees only their data + explicitly shared budgets
- No snooping — PIN-protected profiles stay locked until authenticated

**Security Details**:

- SHA-256 PIN hashing with unique salt per profile
- Configurable failed attempt lockout (prevents brute force)
- Auto-lock after configurable inactivity period
- Activity audit trail for accountability

**Perfect For**:

- Couples managing finances together but keeping some spending private
- Parents giving kids their own budget tracking space
- Roommates sharing household expenses
- Anyone who values privacy within shared devices

**Benefit Statement**: Share a device, not your secrets. Every family member gets their own private, PIN-protected financial space.
