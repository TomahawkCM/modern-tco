# Competitive Research Report — Baseline — 2026-02-05

## Executive Summary

- Privacy-first budgeting has no dominant player. Actual Budget is the nearest privacy competitor but lacks AI, multi-currency, and breadth.
- YNAB price increase ($84 -> $109/yr) continues to drive churn. Monarch Money is the primary beneficiary.
- AI finance agents are exploding (Mine $14M, Tendi, ElektraFi) but none combine AI with zero-knowledge encryption.
- Section 1033 (US) is effectively on hold; PSD3 (EU) political agreement reached. Passkey adoption at tipping point.
- Our combined moat — E2E encryption + 114 locales + offline-first + 17 AI modules — is unmatched by any single competitor.

## New Competitor Developments

### YNAB ($14.99/mo or $109/yr)
- **New features**: Spotlight (attention dashboard), UK Open Banking via Apple Wallet, loan planner, templates, cost-of-living calculator, mobile redesign, duplicate account detection
- **Pricing changes**: Raised to $109/yr (from $99), sharing with up to 6 people included
- **User sentiment**: Gold standard for zero-based budgeting; price complaints persist but loyalty strong
- **Privacy stance**: No E2E encryption, cloud-stored data readable by YNAB

### Monarch Money ($14.99/mo or $99.99/yr)
- **New features**: AI Assistant, reimagined goals, equity tracking, receipt scanning, retail sync extension (Amazon + Target), credit score tracking, connection health dashboard, "yours/mine/ours" couple labels
- **Pricing changes**: Promotional $50/yr for new users (NEWYEAR2026), no free tier
- **User sentiment**: Rapidly growing, considered "YNAB with superpowers"; couples features and investment tracking praised
- **Privacy stance**: No E2E encryption, US-centric, no offline mode

### Copilot Money ($13/mo or $95/yr)
- **New features**: Transaction AI categorization, budget rebalancing, web app launched, Amazon/Venmo integrations, improved bank connections
- **Pricing changes**: No change; premium pricing justified by Apple Editor's Choice
- **User sentiment**: Loved on Apple ecosystem; Android users frustrated (STILL not available, promised since 2023)
- **Privacy stance**: Cloud-stored, standard security

### Rocket Money ($7-14/mo pay-what-you-want)
- **New features**: Custom categories, transaction rules, chat with expert, smart savings account
- **Pricing changes**: No change
- **User sentiment**: 10M+ users; subscription cancellation + bill negotiation moat ($2.5B claimed savings)
- **Privacy stance**: Standard cloud, data used for service features

### Simplifi by Quicken ($3.99-5.99/mo)
- **New features**: Advanced automation rules, customizable dashboard, Kelley Blue Book vehicle tracking, credit score (VantageScore 3.0), LifeHub bundle
- **Pricing changes**: Most affordable mainstream option ($47.88-71.88/yr)
- **User sentiment**: Cash flow projection praised as standout; Quicken brand baggage
- **Privacy stance**: Cloud-stored, standard security

### Actual Budget (Free self-host / $7/mo cloud)
- **New features**: Plugin system (first plugins = bank sync providers), goals UI improvements
- **Pricing changes**: No change
- **User sentiment**: Growing open-source community, YNAB refugees adopting
- **Privacy stance**: Self-hosted = full control; cloud = optional E2E encryption (password-derived keys)

### Lunch Money ($5+/mo PWYW, min $60/yr from Mar 2026)
- **New features**: Continued multi-currency refinement, automation rules, developer API improvements
- **Pricing changes**: Minimum annual price increasing to $60/yr (from flexible PWYW)
- **User sentiment**: Growing in expat/digital nomad market; solo-developer transparency valued
- **Privacy stance**: Cloud-stored, 100% customer-funded (no investors, no data monetization)

### Emerging AI Finance Entrants
- **Mine/MoneyGPT**: $14M Series A (Jan 2026), AI agent learning spending habits, targeting young adults
- **Tendi.ai**: AI financial advisor, Financial Health Index (0-100), trained on CFP exam content
- **Arta AI**: Agentic investment system with RAG + function calling + convex optimization
- **ElektraFi**: Unprompted recommendations monitoring account data
- **Market trend**: AI personal finance $1B (2025) -> projected $3.7B by 2033 (18.1% CAGR)

## Regulatory Updates

- **Section 1033 (US)**: Effectively on hold. Court injunction (Oct 2025), CFPB reversing course, new rulemaking in progress. Original April 2026 deadline will NOT be met. Do NOT depend on this for bank sync strategy.
- **PSD3 (EU)**: Political agreement reached (Nov 2025), formal adoption expected mid-2026, compliance likely H2 2027 or early 2028. Standardized APIs across EU member states.
- **Canadian Consumer-Driven Banking**: Framework progressing; moving faster than US. Build architecture to support.
- **GDPR**: Ongoing relevance for EU users. Cloud sync must respect data residency.
- **Passkeys/FIDO2**: At tipping point — 87% of companies deploying or planning. Regulatory mandates: UAE, India, Philippines (2026). Gartner: main auth method by 2027. COSE standard now includes post-quantum Dilithium signatures.

## Emerging Threats & Opportunities

**Threats**:
- AI-first finance apps (Mine $14M Series A) could leapfrog traditional budgeting tools with conversational UX
- Neobank super-apps (Revolut, Monzo) embedding better budgeting features could make standalone apps less necessary
- Actual Budget plugin system could accelerate its feature gap closure
- Copilot Money's eventual Android launch would remove a major acquisition channel for us

**Opportunities**:
- YNAB price fatigue continues to push users toward alternatives — privacy story resonates with this audience
- Copilot's missing Android creates a large underserved user segment
- No competitor combines AI + privacy + multi-currency + offline — we can own the intersection
- Canadian market has zero dedicated budgeting tools with tax-advantaged account support
- Expat/digital nomad market growing — multi-currency + multi-locale is a natural fit
- Self-hosting demand growing (post-YNAB-price-hike, post-Mint-shutdown)

## Recommended Phase Adjustments

No phase order changes recommended at baseline. The current phase sequence (Foundation -> Bank Sync -> Family -> AI -> Wealth -> Platform -> Tax -> Polish -> Growth) is well-structured. Specific observations:

1. **Phase 2 (Bank Sync)** should include Lunch Money-style automation rules earlier if feasible — Simplifi and Lunch Money both shipped this and it's a growing user expectation.
2. **Phase 4 (AI)** urgency is increasing given Mine/MoneyGPT funding. Consider accelerating basic AI coach to Phase 2/3 as a differentiator.
3. **Phase 9 (Self-Hosted)** could be pulled forward if Actual Budget's plugin system accelerates their growth — monitor quarterly.
