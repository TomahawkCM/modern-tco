# Getting Started with Budget App

Welcome to your personal budget companion! This guide will help you get started managing your finances with confidence.

---

## What is Budget App?

Budget App is a free, private tool that helps you:

- **Track your spending** - See where your money goes
- **Create budgets** - Plan your spending for each category
- **Manage loans** - Track mortgages, car loans, and credit cards
- **Import bank statements** - Bring in your transactions automatically
- **Stay organized** - Keep all your finances in one secure place

**Your data stays on your device** - We don't send your financial information anywhere.

## Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Add Your First Transaction

1. **Open the app** and click the blue **"Add Transaction"** button
2. **Fill in the details**:
   - **Amount**: How much you spent (e.g., $45.50)
   - **Description**: What you bought (e.g., "Groceries at Safeway")
   - **Category**: Pick from the list (e.g., "Groceries")
   - **Date**: When you bought it (defaults to today)
3. **Click "Save"**

That's it! Your first transaction is now tracked.

### Step 2: See Your Spending

Go to the **Dashboard** (home icon at the bottom) to see:

- **How much you've spent** this month
- **Your top spending categories** (colorful pie chart)
- **Recent transactions** (last 10)

### Step 3: Create Your First Budget (Optional)

1. **Tap "Budgets"** in the bottom menu
2. **Click "Create Budget"**
3. **Choose a category** (e.g., "Dining Out")
4. **Set your limit** (e.g., $200/month)
5. **Pick the time period** (Monthly, Weekly, or Yearly)
6. **Save!**

Now you'll see a progress bar showing how much of your budget you've used.

## Categories Explained

Budget App groups your spending into categories like:

- **Groceries** - Food and household items
- **Dining Out** - Restaurants and takeout
- **Transportation** - Gas, parking, public transit
- **Utilities** - Electricity, water, internet
- **Entertainment** - Movies, streaming, hobbies

**Why categories matter**:

- See spending patterns at a glance
- Create budgets for each category
- Identify areas to cut back

### Auto-Categorization

The app tries to **guess the category** based on your description:

- Type "Starbucks" → Suggests "Dining Out"
- Type "Shell gas" → Suggests "Transportation"
- Type "Netflix" → Suggests "Entertainment"

You can always change the category if the guess is wrong.

## Creating Budgets

### What is a Budget?

A budget is a **spending limit** for a category. For example:

- "I want to spend no more than $300/month on groceries"
- "I'll limit dining out to $150/month"

### How to Create a Budget

1. Go to **Budgets** section
2. Click **"Create Budget"**
3. **Choose your settings**:
   - **Category**: What you're budgeting for
   - **Amount**: Your spending limit (e.g., $300)
   - **Period**: Monthly, Weekly, or Yearly
   - **Start Date**: When to begin tracking
4. **Click "Save"**

### Tracking Your Budget

Your budget shows:

- **Progress bar**: Green (under budget), Yellow (close), Red (over)
- **Amount spent / Total budget** (e.g., "$245 / $300")
- **Remaining amount** (e.g., "$55 left")
- **Days left** in the period

**Pro Tip**: Start with just 2-3 budgets for your biggest spending categories. Add more as you get comfortable.

## Importing Bank Statements

Save time by importing your bank statements!

### Supported Formats

- **CSV files** (from most banks)
- **OFX/QFX files** (Quicken format)
- **PDF bank statements** (coming soon)

### How to Import

1. **Download your statement** from your bank's website
   - Look for "Download Transactions" or "Export"
   - Choose CSV or OFX format

2. **Open Budget App** → Go to **More** → **Import**

3. **Select your bank** from the list (or choose "Generic CSV")

4. **Upload the file**:
   - Click "Choose File"
   - Select your downloaded statement
   - Click "Upload"

5. **Preview your transactions**:
   - Review the import (shows first 20)
   - Check for duplicates (marked in yellow)
   - Click "Import" to confirm

### Duplicate Detection

The app **automatically finds duplicates** by matching:

- Same date (±1 day)
- Same amount
- Similar description

**Duplicates are marked** but NOT imported to keep your data clean.

## Managing Loans

Track mortgages, car loans, and credit cards to see your **debt payoff progress**.

### Adding a Loan

1. Go to **Loans** section
2. Click **"Add Loan"**
3. **Fill in the details**:
   - **Name**: "Mortgage" or "Car Loan"
   - **Type**: Mortgage, Auto, Credit Card, Personal
   - **Principal**: Original loan amount (e.g., $200,000)
   - **Interest Rate**: Annual % (e.g., 4.5%)
   - **Term**: Years to pay off (e.g., 30)
   - **Start Date**: When you got the loan
   - **Monthly Payment**: Your payment amount
4. **Click "Save"**

### Loan Dashboard

For each loan, you'll see:

- **Remaining balance**
- **Monthly payment** amount
- **Total interest paid** so far
- **Payoff date**
- **Amortization chart** (shows principal vs interest over time)

### Extra Payments Calculator

Want to pay off your loan faster?

1. **Open a loan**
2. **Click "Extra Payments"** tab
3. **Move the slider** to test different extra payment amounts
4. **See the impact**:
   - New payoff date
   - Total interest saved
   - Years saved

**Example**: Paying $200 extra per month on a 30-year mortgage could save you 8 years and $50,000 in interest!

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Load

**Try these steps**:

1. **Refresh the page** (F5 or Ctrl+R)
2. **Clear browser cache**: Settings → Privacy → Clear Cache
3. **Try a different browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
4. **Check internet connection** (for first load only)

### Transactions Not Showing

**Check**:

1. **Date filters** - Make sure you're viewing the right time period
2. **Category filters** - Clear any active filters
3. **Scroll down** - Older transactions are at the bottom

### Import Failed

**Common issues**:

1. **Wrong file format** - Use CSV or OFX, not PDF
2. **Missing required columns** - File must have Date, Amount, Description
3. **Large file size** - Files over 5MB are rejected. Split into smaller files.

### Chatbot Not Responding

**Check**:

1. **Internet connection** - Chatbot requires internet
2. **Ad blockers** - Disable for this site
3. **Privacy mode** - Some browsers block AI features in private mode

### Data Disappeared

**Your data is safe!** Try:

1. **Refresh the page** - Data is cached locally
2. **Check browser storage** - Settings → Privacy → Site Data
3. **Look for backups** - More → Settings → Export Data
