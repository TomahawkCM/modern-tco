# Online Budget App — V1 Onboarding & Activation Funnel

Status: Planning
Last Updated: 2026-02-22
Scope: V1 (Strict)
Goal: Fast activation, high retention, minimal friction

---

# 1. Activation Philosophy

Activation for V1 is NOT:

- Completing profile
- Setting up 10 budgets
- Exploring every feature

Activation IS:

> User connects a bank AND receives their first meaningful AI insight.

Time target:
Under 2 minutes from sign-up to first "aha" moment.

---

# 2. Funnel Overview

Stage 1: Entry
Stage 2: Account Creation
Stage 3: Bank Connection
Stage 4: Data Processing (Invisible Work)
Stage 5: First Insight Reveal
Stage 6: Guided Dashboard Tour
Stage 7: First Budget or Goal Setup (Optional)

Primary KPI:
% of users who connect a bank and see first AI insight.

---

# 3. Stage-by-Stage Design

---

## Stage 1 — Landing Page (Pre-Signup)

Objective:
Communicate value in 10 seconds.

Messaging Focus:

- AI-powered financial clarity
- Global bank connectivity
- Multi-currency support
- Clean & private

Primary CTA:
"See My Money Clearly"

Avoid:

- Long feature lists
- Overpromising AI
- Complex pricing explanation

---

## Stage 2 — Account Creation

Keep minimal:

- Email
- Password (or OAuth)

After signup:
Immediately route to bank connection.

Do NOT ask for:

- Full profile
- Address
- Income
- Budget setup

Friction kills activation.

---

## Stage 3 — Bank Connection (Critical Step)

UI must:

- Explain security clearly
- Show supported banks
- Indicate multi-currency support

Psychological reinforcement:
"This lets us analyze your spending instantly."

If user skips:
Offer manual mode but explain:
"AI insights require transaction data."

---

## Stage 4 — Data Processing Screen

While transactions import:

Display:

- "Analyzing your spending patterns..."
- Subtle animation
- Educational microcopy (e.g., "We automatically categorize transactions.")

Keep wait under 20–30 seconds.
If longer:
Allow background processing + notify when ready.

---

## Stage 5 — First Insight Reveal (Aha Moment)

Immediately show:

Card 1:
"You spent $X last month."

Card 2:
"Your biggest category was Y."

Card 3:
"Your savings rate was Z%."

Optional:
"You have 3 recurring subscriptions."

This must feel personalized and intelligent.

---

## Stage 6 — Guided Dashboard Tour

Short, 4-step overlay:

1. This is your balance overview
2. This shows your spending categories
3. This is your savings rate
4. Ask AI anything about your money

Allow skip anytime.

---

## Stage 7 — First Budget or Goal Prompt

After insight reveal:

Prompt:
"Would you like to set a monthly budget for your top spending category?"

OR

"Want to create a savings goal?"

Make optional.
Never block access.

---

# 4. AI Activation Strategy

During first session:

Trigger conversational prompt:
"Ask me something about your spending."

Pre-fill suggestions:

- "How much did I spend on food?"
- "Can I afford a $400 payment?"

First AI interaction strongly predicts retention.

---

# 5. Drop-Off Risk Points

High-risk areas:

1. Bank connection hesitation
2. Long import time
3. Overwhelming dashboard
4. No immediate value after login

Mitigation:

- Clear security messaging
- Fast feedback loops
- Clean UI
- Immediate insights

---

# 6. Activation Metrics

Primary:

- % who connect a bank
- % who see first insight
- % who interact with AI in first session

Secondary:

- Time to first insight
- Time to first budget creation

---

# 7. Retention Loop

Weekly Loop:

- AI generates summary
- Notification sent
- User opens app
- Sees new insight
- Asks question

Monthly Loop:

- Spending report
- Savings progress update
- Goal milestone

Consistency builds habit.

---

# 8. What We Avoid in V1 Onboarding

- Long questionnaires
- Financial jargon
- Forced budgeting setup
- Advanced modeling setup
- Complex feature education

Keep it calm.
Keep it simple.

---

# 9. Success Criteria

Activation target:
60%+ of signups connect a bank.

First-session AI interaction target:
40%+ of activated users.

If below:
Simplify flow immediately.

---

# Final Principle

Onboarding is not about features.
It is about delivering clarity fast.

If users see their money clearly within 2 minutes,
retention probability increases dramatically.
