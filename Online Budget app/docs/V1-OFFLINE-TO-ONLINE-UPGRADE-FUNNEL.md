# Offline → Online Upgrade Funnel (V1)

Status: Planning
Last Updated: 2026-02-22
Goal: Convert Offline Users into Online Premium Subscribers

---

# 1. Strategic Positioning

Offline = Control + Modeling
Online = Automation + Intelligence

We do NOT position Online as "better".
We position it as:

> "Let it run automatically for you."

Offline users already value financial control.
Online removes friction and adds automation.

---

# 2. Upgrade Philosophy

The upgrade must feel like:

- Relief from manual work
- Increased visibility
- Smarter insights
- Cross-device convenience

It must NOT feel like:

- Feature gating
- Forced monetization
- Punishment for using free version

---

# 3. Trigger Moments for Upgrade

Upgrade offers should appear at high-friction points in Offline:

1. Manual CSV Import (after 2–3 imports)
   Message: "Tired of importing? Connect your bank automatically."

2. Recurring Transaction Detection
   "Want subscriptions tracked automatically?"

3. After Generating a Financial Report
   "Get monthly AI summaries automatically."

4. Multi-Device Use Case
   "Access your data across devices instantly."

5. Missed Import Reminder
   "Stay up to date automatically with Online."

---

# 4. Funnel Stages

Stage 1 — Awareness

In Offline app:

- Small "Online" badge in navigation
- "Automate this" inline prompts
- Non-intrusive banner after manual tasks

---

Stage 2 — Education Page

Dedicated landing inside Offline:

Title:
"Stop Importing. Let It Sync Automatically."

Explain:

- Automatic bank sync
- AI insights
- Real-time updates
- Secure & encrypted

Include:

- Short demo GIF
- Security reassurance

---

Stage 3 — Trial Activation

Offer:
7–14 day free trial

No credit card upfront (optional A/B test)
OR
Credit card required (reduces abuse, increases commitment)

Goal:
User connects bank immediately during trial.

---

Stage 4 — First Online Aha Moment

Within first 2 minutes:

- Bank connected
- AI generates first insight
- Dashboard auto-populated

This must outperform Offline in convenience.

---

Stage 5 — Conversion Prompt (Before Trial Ends)

3 days before expiration:
"Your accounts will stop syncing soon."

Show value recap:

- X insights generated
- Y transactions auto-categorized
- Z hours saved

Then present pricing clearly.

---

# 5. Pricing Psychology

Recommended:
$9–$12/month

OR
$99/year (2 months free)

Annual plan improves cash flow and reduces churn.

---

# 6. Conversion Benchmarks

Healthy Targets:

- 20–30% of Offline active users try Online trial
- 30–50% of trial users convert

Effective overall Offline → Paid conversion: ~6–10%

---

# 7. Messaging Framework

Core Value Themes:

1. Save Time
2. Stay Updated
3. Get Smart Insights
4. Access Anywhere

Avoid messaging around:

- "More features"
- "Advanced modeling"

Offline already handles power users.
Online handles convenience.

---

# 8. UX Rules

- No aggressive popups
- No blocking features
- Contextual upgrade prompts only
- Always allow dismissal
- Respect user autonomy

Trust drives long-term conversion.

---

# 9. Churn Prevention in Trial

During trial:

- Send weekly insight summary email
- Highlight subscription detection
- Show financial health score

Make Online feel alive.

---

# 10. Failure Mode

If Offline is "good enough" and friction low,
conversion will stall.

To prevent this:

- Do NOT over-optimize manual import
- Keep Offline powerful but effortful
- Let Online feel effortless

---

# Final Strategy Summary

Offline builds trust.
Online removes effort.

Upgrade is driven by convenience and automation,
not by restricting capability.

This maintains goodwill while driving sustainable subscription growth.
