# MDX Examples

<Callout type="tip" title="Analogy Anchor">
Tanium is like a city-wide radio network: one message reaches every station instantly.
</Callout>

<InfoBox title="Key Term" theme="definition">
**Endpoint** — Any computer, server, or device managed by Tanium. Think of it as a citizen in your city.
</InfoBox>

<Steps>
  <StepItem title="Open the Console">
    Sign into Tanium and choose **Interact** from the main navigation.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Ask your first question">
    Select *Question Builder*, pick **Computer Name**, then click **Ask Question**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Review live results">
    Watch the response list populate across all reachable endpoints.
  </StepItem>
</Steps>
